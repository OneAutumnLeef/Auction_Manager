# Rules

## Team Formation
- Each team must consist of **11 players**:
  - Minimum **3 batsmen**
  - Minimum **2 all-rounders**
  - Minimum **3 bowlers**
  - **1 wicketkeeper**
  - At most **4 foreign players**
- If any team fails to meet these criteria, they will be **disqualified**.

## Winner Selection
- **The team with the highest overall rating will be declared the winner.**

## Bidding Rules
- Once you **bid for a player, you are committed** unless outbid.
- If a team **runs out of money, they are disqualified**.
- Players are **categorized** into **A, B, and C** categories, and the category cannot be changed.
- **Bidding Increments**:
  - **Till 50 lakh:** bid increases by **10 lakh**.
  - **From 50 lakh to 1 crore:** bid increases by **25 lakh**.
  - **Above 1 crore:** bid increases by **50 lakh**.

## Tie-Breaker
- In case of a tie, the team with the **highest remaining budget** will be declared the winner.

## Other Guidelines
- The **judges' decision is final and indisputable**.
- Participants are expected to **maintain appropriate behavior** throughout the event.
